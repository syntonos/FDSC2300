## Fonts

Cormorant Garamond and DM Sans come from the official [Google Fonts repository](https://github.com/google/fonts), under the SIL Open Font License. Their license files are included in `public/fonts`. Fonts are served locally.

## Organization

Store new course images under `public/images/<category>/` and reference them in dish Markdown as `/images/<category>/<filename>`. Image categories are ordinary folders and can be added freely. The dish's textual `category` field controls archive filtering independently of its image folder.
