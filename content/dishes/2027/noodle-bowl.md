## Science

Noodles absorb water as their starch gelatinizes. Continued contact with hot broth changes their texture, while broth extraction and seasoning determine flavor.

## Method

Read the assigned recipe in Canvas and plan the cooking order. Check allergens, including wheat, eggs, shellfish, sesame, and soy. Follow the prescribed safe cooking procedures for shrimp and eggs. Assemble noodles, broth, vegetables, and toppings just before serving. Compare freshly cooked noodles with a portion left in broth for several minutes.
