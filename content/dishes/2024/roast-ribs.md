## Science

The surface of a roast browns while heat moves toward its center. Muscle proteins and connective tissue respond differently to heat; the cut determines the appropriate cooking method. A browned crust does not establish a safe internal temperature.

## Method

Follow the instructor’s method for the selected cut. Keep raw meat separate from ready-to-eat ingredients. Check the prescribed safe internal temperature with a thermometer, placing the probe away from bone. Rest as instructed before carving.
