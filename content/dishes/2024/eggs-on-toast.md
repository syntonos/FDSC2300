## Science

Heat unfolds egg proteins, which connect to form a structure. Whites and yolks set differently, while the bread loses surface moisture and develops toasted flavors through browning reactions.

## Method

Toast the bread just before serving. Cook eggs using the instructor’s food safety procedure; use pasteurized eggs when a preparation calls for a less-set yolk. Finish with herbs and pepper. Compare the texture of the toast before and after adding the egg.
