## Science

Heat unfolds egg proteins, allowing them to form a network that holds water and fat. Sugar and acidity affect this process. Gentle heat and frequent stirring help the curd thicken evenly without becoming grainy.

## Method

Bake the pastry shell until dry and golden, then cool. Warm the curd gradually, watching it change from fluid to a coating consistency. Follow the instructor’s temperature guidance, strain, and chill before slicing. Compare the curd’s acidity with the pastry’s richness.
