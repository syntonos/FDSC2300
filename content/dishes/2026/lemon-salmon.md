## Science

Heat changes fish proteins, affecting opacity, flaking, and moisture retention. Color and flaking alone cannot confirm that the fish has reached a safe internal temperature.

## Method

Review the assigned preparation in Canvas. Keep salmon refrigerated until needed and use separate tools for raw and cooked fish. Follow the instructor’s cooking method and verify the prescribed safe temperature with a thermometer. Finish with lemon and herbs; compare their acidity and aroma with the fish’s richness.
