## Science

Cooking softens pumpkin tissue as cell-wall components change. Blending breaks it into smaller particles, whose size and concentration affect the purée’s texture. Adding stock changes the balance of liquid and solids, making the soup thinner.

## Method

Cut pumpkin into similar-sized pieces and cook until fully tender. Follow the blender’s guidance for hot liquids. Add stock gradually, checking how the soup falls from a spoon. Season and finish with seeds, cheese, and spice.
