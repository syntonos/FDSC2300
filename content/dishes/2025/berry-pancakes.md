## Science

Chemical leaveners produce gas that expands with heat. Starch gelatinizes and proteins set around the bubbles, giving pancakes their structure. Excessive mixing develops flour proteins and can make the crumb less tender.

## Method

Mix only until the dry flour disappears. Keep the pan evenly heated. Watch the bubbles, edges, and underside before turning. Serve with berries, mint, and honey. Cut a pancake to examine its crumb, comparing the tender center with the browned surface.
