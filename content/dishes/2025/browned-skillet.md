## Science

Maillard reactions between reducing sugars and amino compounds create browned flavors. Surface moisture limits temperature, so ingredients must lose water before browning develops. Overcrowding traps moisture and slows the process.

## Method

Work in batches if necessary. Let ingredients brown before stirring. Cook meat to the prescribed safe internal temperature. Add a measured amount of liquid to loosen browned deposits, then reduce gently. Taste near the end: reduction concentrates both flavor and seasoning.
