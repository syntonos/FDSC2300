---
title: Kitchen essentials
description: Preparation, safe handling, and cleanup.
category: Practice
order: 1
---

- Read the recipe first. Weigh ingredients and prepare your tools.
- Tie back hair, wear closed-toe shoes, and wash hands before cooking and between tasks.
- Separate raw and ready-to-eat foods. Use a thermometer and the prescribed safe internal temperatures.
- Check allergens and label ingredients, contents, and preparation dates.
- Secure cutting boards. Announce hot pans and use dry oven mitts.
- Record quantities, temperatures, and timing. Refrigerate perishables promptly; clean and sanitize your station before leaving.

Follow your instructor’s lab procedures.
