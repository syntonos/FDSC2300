---
title: Heat transfer
description: How energy reaches the food we cook.
category: Food science
order: 2
---

**Conduction** transfers energy through direct contact: a pan heats the surface of a pancake.

**Convection** moves energy through liquids or gases: circulating water cooks vegetables, and oven air heats a roasting tray.

**Radiation** transfers energy without direct contact: a broiler browns an exposed surface.

Most cooking uses more than one mechanism. Note the equipment, surface temperature, food thickness, and cooking time when comparing results. Use the prescribed safe internal temperature to confirm doneness.
