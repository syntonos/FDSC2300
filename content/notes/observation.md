---
title: Observation notes
description: Record enough detail to repeat an experiment.
category: Practice
order: 3
---

Record ingredient quantities, temperatures, timing, and one change at a time.

Describe aroma, color, texture, and flavor with specific comparisons. “More acidic after adding 5 g of lemon juice” is easier to act on than “better.”

Photograph the finished dish and credit the people who made it. Add the photo and a short caption to the dish’s student-work gallery.
